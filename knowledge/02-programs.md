# Academic Programs at E-JUST

E-JUST offers undergraduate and postgraduate (M.Sc. and Ph.D.) programs across several specialized faculties and schools.

## School of Engineering
- Electronics and Communications Engineering (ECE)
- Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)
- Mechatronics and Robotics Engineering (MTR)
- Industrial and Manufacturing Engineering (IME)
- Materials Science and Engineering (MSE)
- Energy Resources and Environmental Engineering (ERE)
- Chemical and Petrochemical Engineering (CPE)
- Sustainable Architecture (ARC)

## Faculty of Computer Science and Information Technology (CSIT)
- Artificial Intelligence and Data Science
- Computer Science
- Cybersecurity and Networks
- Bioinformatics and Computational Systems

## Faculty of International Business and Humanities (FIBH)
- Accounting and Information Systems
- Human Resources Management
- Marketing and International Business

## Postgraduate Programs (M.Sc. & Ph.D.)
- Research-intensive graduate degrees supervised jointly by Egyptian and Japanese partner university faculty (including Tokyo Tech, Kyushu University, Kyoto University, Waseda, Osaka, and others).
