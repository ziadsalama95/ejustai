## Personal Info

Received the B.Sc. degree (Hons.) in Computer engineering, in 2009, the M.Sc. degree from Cairo University in Computer Engineering, Cairo, Egypt, in 2015, and the Ph.D. degree from the School of Electronics, Communications, and Computer Science Engineering, Egypt-Japan University of Science and Technology (E-JUST), in collaboration with Kyushu University, Japan in 2020. From 2009 to 2015, he was a Teaching Assistant at IAET. In 2015, he joined National Research Institute of Astronomy and Geophysics (NRIAG), Egypt. In February 2019, he has joined the Laboratory for Image and Media Understanding (LIMU), Kyushu University, Japan, as a Special Research Student. From 2022-2023, he has joined the Laboratory for Image and Media Understanding (LIMU), Kyushu University, Japan, where he had a POST-DOC Fellowship. He is currently an Assistant Professor in Computer Science and Engineering department, with the Egypt Japan University of Science and Technology (E-JUST). Received top honors in entrepreneurship competition for the innovative academic business idea, recognized by National Research Institute of Astronomy and Geophysics, 2024. His current research interests include Artificial Intelligence, Image & Video Processing, Computer Vision, Deep Learning, Global Navigation Satellite Systems (GNSS), Remote Sensing, Wireless Sensor Networks, Intelligent transportation systems, and Object Detection & Tracking.
