## A national pathway for exceptional young mathematical talent.

The Egypt-Japan University of Science and Technology (E-JUST) is pleased to announce that it will serve as the national coordinating institution for Egypt's participation in the International Mathematical Olympiad (IMO), in cooperation with the Egyptian Ministry of Education and Technical Education.

Following official communication between the Ministry and the President of the IMO Board, Egypt has been welcomed back to the international competition, with E-JUST entrusted with coordinating the national participation and student selection process.

This initiative aims to identify and nurture exceptional mathematical talent among Egyptian high school students and provide them with the opportunity to represent Egypt in one of the world's most prestigious international academic competitions.

## The International Mathematical Olympiad

The IMO is the world's leading international mathematics competition for high school students. Established in 1959, it brings together talented young mathematicians from more than 100 countries.

The competition challenges students through advanced mathematical problems requiring creativity, logical reasoning, and rigorous mathematical proofs. Each participating country may send a team of up to six students.

## How Egypt's IMO 2027 team will be selected.

E-JUST will coordinate a nationwide selection process to identify the six students who will represent Egypt at IMO 2027. The proposed process consists of two stages.

### National Online Qualifying Examination

- Open to eligible high school students across Egypt.

- Conducted online, allowing nationwide participation without the need to travel.

- Expected to attract approximately 2,000 students.

- Tentatively scheduled during the winter school break of 2027.

- The top 30 students will qualify for the final selection round.

The proposed registration fee is EGP 400, with need-based fee waivers available to support equal opportunities. The fee may be reduced if external sponsorship is secured. Final registration conditions will be announced before applications open.

### National Final Examination at E-JUST

- The top 30 students from the online qualifying round will be invited to E-JUST for an in-person final examination.

- The final examination is expected to take place approximately two to three weeks after the online qualifying round.

- No additional registration fee will be required from the 30 finalists.

- The six highest-performing students, according to the approved selection criteria, will be selected to represent Egypt at IMO 2027.

## Preparing Egypt's national team.

Following the selection process, E-JUST will coordinate a specialized mathematical training program for the six selected students.

The program will focus on advanced problem-solving techniques and the mathematical areas covered by the IMO.

Training will initially be provided online, with opportunities for additional in-person workshops or training camps subject to the availability of external funding.

The official Egyptian delegation will consist of six students, a Team Leader, and a Deputy Team Leader.
