# About

The mission and vision of E-JUST Library align with the university's goals of supporting academic excellence, fostering research, expanding knowledge, and promoting problem-solving and critical thinking skills.

### Mission

The mission of E-JUST Library is to facilitate learning by providing diverse, accessible, and up-to-date information resources and services that support education, research, and the scholarly needs of its users. Additionally, we are committed to addressing other aspects of students' development by expanding their knowledge and enhancing their skills for both daily life and academic pursuits. The library strives to foster learning, creativity, and innovation through effective resource management, creating an environment that supports the varied needs of individuals and groups.

### Vision

Our vision is to become a leading center of knowledge and information, both in Egypt and globally, by:

- Providing cutting-edge digital and physical resources.

- Utilizing advanced technology to enhance the delivery of services and information.

- Ensuring that the community we serve has the tools needed in the rapidly evolving era of technology and information.

- Fostering a culture of continuous learning to help individuals adapt to new challenges and enhance their skills in an ever-changing world.

- Building strong partnerships with internal and global institutions, universities, libraries, research centers, and organizations.

- Encouraging sustainable practices and increasing awareness.
