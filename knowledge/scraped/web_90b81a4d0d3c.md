An official contract has been signed to transfer the research outcome EyeHealthy from lab to Market. The EyeHealthy lamp represents the first industrial outcome from the scientific research conducted by my research group at the Japan University of Science and Technology, led by Professor Mohsen Ghali from the Faculty of Basic and Applied Sciences. It should be noted that the EyeHealthy lamp is an energy-saving LED lamp, distinguished by being free from the harmful effects of intense blue light glare, which is prevalent in traditional white LED bulbs (daylight).
