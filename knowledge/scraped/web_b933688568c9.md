### Incubator Technology

Egypt-Japan University of Science and Technology (E-JUST) is a research university with an ambition to cultivate an academic environment and become a benchmark for the Egyptian and African countries in education. It was first established as a bilateral agreement project between the Egyptian and Japanese governments in May 2009 and later in 2010 it was ready to accept its first batch of students and make the dream a reality.

The university has been pioneering new interdisciplinary fields for providing technologies required to create a sustainable society in Egypt, Africa, and Arab countries. Therefore, EJUST want to provide its student with the opportunities to innovate and start their own business based on their innovation and become leading entrepreneurs. EJUST also wants to commercialize its staff research outputs through setting up a new or “spin-off” companies based on these emerging innovations under licenses from the University.

- Creating jobs

- Encouraging social innovation

- Satisfying sustainable development goals

- Incubating social startups

- Provide training on social entrepreneurship

Incubating spin-off companies create an entrepreneurial environment in the University that encourages and rewards the generation of new inventions and knowledge, and facilitate the transfer of new commercially and socially important inventions and knowledge to society. Businesses and individuals can collaborate with EJUST to develop research with commercial potential or invest in EJUST spin-off companies.

Companies can also spin-in to EJUST and access university inventions, research, technologies and facilities to develop a commercial opportunity through a collaborative approach in return for an equity stake. The key criteria for assessment of the spin-in opportunity are alignment with the key research objectives of the university and an opportunity for collaborative research and integration of the university’s expertise and resources.

For these reasons the university decided to establish its own innovation ecosystem starting by establishing a technology incubator as a platform for incubating innovative ideas, registered patents’ owners, and researchers.

E-JUST virtual incubator is dedicated to provide incubation service remotely and create a network for open innovative and investment opportunities. Dragon Incubator support product development startups where research outputs and technological needs are transferred into new products that can be introduce to the market. The product can be tangible (something physical which one can touch) or intangible (like a service or experience). Dragon Incubator also focuses on increasing the TRL of E-JUST research outcomes to marketable level through whole process of product development. Dragon Incubator has been specifically designed to attract new and distinctive ideas. The incubator enabling startup teams to scale their business, redesigned their ideas and gives them a support to develop and become selfsustained.

### Dragon Innovation Club

Transform academic research and student projects into commercial reality by bridging university talent with actual industry and startup needs.

Establish the "Dragon Innovation Lab" as the premier R&D engine for local enterprises and the launchpad for high-growth student startups.

To motivate members and guarantee output quality, membership level is directly tied to verified contributions. Members accumulate "Dragon Points" through lab participation, challenge solving, and R&D activities.

Local industrial companies post real engineering/tech challenges. Blue and Green teams (under Red/Gold supervision) solve them for cash prizes, employment offers, or project funding.

The club acts as an external R&D facility for off-campus startups lacking technical equipment. Students develop product components using E-JUST lab infrastructure.

A bi-annual showcase event where project teams pitch their functional prototypes directly to Venture Capitalists (VCs), angel investors, and industrial R&D directors.

### Dragon Levels

A color-coded rank system built on verified contributions and Dragon Points.

Requirements Newly joined + Passed Lab Safety Training + Attended 1 core workshop.

Privileges Standard lab access during operational hours; Join other project teams as Assistant.

Requirements Accumulate 100 Dragon Points + Active role in 1 industrial project or startup team.

Privileges Priority equipment booking; Direct eligibility for startup assignment; Prototype funding support.

Requirements Accumulate 300 Dragon Points + Lead a team to solve a full industry challenge + Deliver working prototype.

Privileges Manage funded projects; Allocated university budget; Direct mentorship from industry leaders.

Requirements Advanced research/practical experience + Technical supervision of teams + Secure industry/research link.

Privileges Dedicated office space; Management of Deep Tech projects; Share in project revenues or patents.

Four Ranks, One Dragon.

Each badge marks a member's rank in the club's color-coded progression system.

### Dragon Points & Promotion

Points alone do not automatically promote a member to "Red Dragon". Candidates who reach 300 points must present a formal pitch before the Promotion Committee (Golden Dragons & Club Admin) presenting their industrial contributions and functional prototype.

Ranks are production-contingent. If a Blue or Red Dragon ceases active lab production or industry project involvement for a full academic semester, 50 points are deducted. If balance drops below threshold, the rank is demoted accordingly.

Faculty and postgraduate researchers enter directly at the Golden level based on academic merit. They do not compete for points; rather, they grant points, evaluate pitches, and guide complex projects exceeding undergraduate scope.

If a Green Dragon successfully secures external investment for a startup or registers an official patent, they immediately skip Blue Dragon level and advance straight to Red Dragon status.

### Governance & University Incentives

IP belongs to the sponsoring firm, while students retain resume presentation rights. Independent projects remain student/university IP per E-JUST regulations.

Members at Blue Dragon level and above must sign a standard Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) to protect corporate partner data.

Red and Golden Dragons must deliver at least one tangible outcome per semester (prototype, paper, or industrial partnership) to retain status.

Safety violations or leaving equipment uncleaned result in immediate rank demotion and forfeiture of university incentives.

University support funds are converted into micro-grants disbursed exclusively for purchasing project prototype components and hardware.

Coordinated with university management to align active participation in Blue/Red projects with Capstone/Graduation Project hours or mandatory field training credits.

### Specialization Tracks (Elemental Dragons)

The Chimera Protocol (Cross-Faculty Mandate): to qualify for Red Dragon status (Project Leader), a student's project team MUST comprise members from at least two different E-JUST faculties. Engineering students build prototypes, business students build financial models, computer science students code software, and applied science students validate materials.
