Our main campus is on 200 acres, it’s like a small city, and designed by Japanese company to be unique eco-friendly fully connected campus. In fact, through an array of programs we, at the residential life, make sure that living and learning happen in harmony.

- Fully furnished room/apartment

- Community lounge/kitchenette per residential unit

- High-speed Wi-Fi internet

- Air conditioning

- Laundry room

- Medical services 24/7

In order for our students to keep a healthy mind in a healthy body, we support them to enjoy physical activity. E-JUST has a one of a kind sports facility. The Activities Hub building includes a heated indoor swimming pool, a squash court and fully equipped gymnasium and locker rooms. Our campus also includes four football fields, two tennis courts and a multi-purpose sports court.

Food outlets are an integral part of campus life. At E-JUST, there are 4 food courts, including a Japanese restaurant, and coffee shops.

E-JUST is keen to help the international students gain the full understanding of the Egyptian and Japanese culture through many activities such as: field trips, in-house cultural events, Arabic and Japanese language courses, etc.
