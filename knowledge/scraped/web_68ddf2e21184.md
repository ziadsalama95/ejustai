The Mathematics and Statistics Program prepares students for a variety of jobs in data science, engineering, research, and finance by providing them with a strong foundation in mathematical modeling, computational techniques, and statistical analysis.

The mission of the mathematics and Statistics (MAS) program is

- To adopt a learner-centered education approach that aims at building the intellectual capacity of our undergraduates, developing their ability for critical thinking, and encouraging them to pursue a life-long learning career.

- To prepare our undergraduates to make significant contributions to society and lead technology transfer in Egypt.

- To provide leadership in teaching, learning, and research.
