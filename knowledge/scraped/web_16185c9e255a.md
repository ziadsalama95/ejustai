# About Program

The Space Environment and Remote Sensing postgraduate program at Egypt-Japan University of Science and Technology (E-JUST) is a leading research and educational hub dedicated to advancing space science, space environment studies, and remote sensing technologies in Egypt and the region.

Established in alignment with Egypt’s national vision for space exploration and technological development, the program actively supports the strategic objectives of the Egyptian Space Agency (EgSA) and is also aligned with the international state of art research . Through advanced scientific research, international collaboration, and hands-on technical training, the program prepares highly qualified professionals capable of contributing to both national and global space programs.

Our interdisciplinary approach combines theoretical knowledge, applied research, engineering innovation, and industry-focused education to develop the next generation of space scientists and researchers.

## Program Vision

To be a globally recognized center of excellence in Space Environment and Remote Sensing, shaping future leaders and advancing knowledge for a smarter, safer, and more sustainable world

## Program Mission

To provide advanced interdisciplinary education and innovative research in Space Environment and Remote Sensing that meets national, regional, and international needs, while fostering scientific excellence, technological innovation, and global competitiveness. The program aims to prepare highly qualified graduates and researchers capable of advancing sustainable development, and strengthening collaboration with academic, research, and industrial partners in Egypt, Japan, and worldwide
