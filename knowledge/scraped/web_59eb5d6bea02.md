Overview of the Academic Year

E-JUST academic year is divided into two main semesters.

Fall Semester: starts in September and continues for 15 study weeks

Spring Semester: starts in February and continues for 15 study weeks

It may be possible to offer some courses during a Summer semester which is condensed semester that starts in July and continues for 7 study weeks.

Registration and grade announcements are semester-based, and grades are released at the end of each semester. There may also be instances where classes and internships are scheduled on Saturdays or holidays.

Credit System

Coursework at E-JUST is based on the credit system. The number of credits earned per course is determined by the Academic Plan.

Registration of Courses

Course registration is obligatory to attend classes, receive credit and for graduation. Course registration takes place (on the Student Information System; SIS) at the beginning of each semester during the times described in the academic calendar announced at the beginning of each academic year (which specifies the deadlines for registration, add/drop, withdrawal…).

Students must fulfill any pre-requisite for each course (specified in the curriculum) before registration (completing and attending the final exam of its prerequisite course, if any).

Course and Laboratory Attendance

The student is not allowed to attend the final exam of a course unless he/she attended at least 75% of the study hours of the course. Otherwise, the student will be considered as "Forced Withdrawn" and the course will appear in his/her certificate as (FW), and will not be counted in calculating the CGPA.

GENERAL RULES FOR ATTENDANCE (lectures, tutorials, labs)

1- The student must attend the lectures, tutorials, labs at the scheduled time/location and no switching is allowed.

2- The student must enter the lectures, tutorials, labs at the exact time (maximum 10 minutes delay if approved by the instructor).

3- The student must fulfill the criteria for (lectures, tutorials, labs) performance (coat, protective measures, tools, .... ) announced by the Course Instructor and the Teaching Assistants.

4- Use of Mobiles, Tablets, Laptops, .... is prohibited unless permitted by the Course Instructor/ Teaching Assistant to serve an educational role.

5- No student is allowed to leave the (lectures, tutorials, labs) without permission.

6- Mutual respect is mandatory in every aspect in E-JUST

7- The student must follow ALL instructions from the Course Instructor and the Teaching Assistant regarding all activities inside the (lectures, tutorials, labs).

8- Any absence excuses must be submitted and handled by the Student Affairs according to the general rules applied at E-JUST.

VIOLATION OF ANY OF THESE RULES IS NOT ACCEPTABLE AND WILL BE DEALT WITH ACCORDING TO THE STANDARD PROCEDURES APPLIED IN E-JUST

Treatment of Class Absences

E-JUST has clear rules for dealing with absences and requests for excused absences are handled by the Academic Affairs Office.

Academic Advising

E-JUST offers a variety of study support options to support students in their independent study.

An academic advisor will be assigned for each student. The academic advisor is responsible for:

- Advising the student during his course work.

- Clarifying the articles of the bylaw to the student.

- Helping the students in registration and the proper courses selection each semester.

- Helping the student to select the elective courses relevant to the field he wishes to study.

- Recommending any additional complementary courses that, in the opinion of the academic advisor, the student has to take.

- Putting the student whose GPA is below 2.00 under observation and trying to help him to raise his GPA by proper courses selection.

- Helping the student in add/drop courses and withdrawal.

Probation and Dismissal

A student whose CGPA is less than 2.00 will be Under Probation :

- Can Register only 9 - 14 credit hours during Fall and/or Spring semesters .

- To end under probation period your Cumulative GPA must raise to 2.00 or more.

- allowed to stay under probation for a maximum of 6 successive semesters after which he/she will be academically dismissed from EJUST.

Registration Advising

Advising for course registration process is available at the Academic Affairs Office and the Academic Advisor.

Course evaluation:

Midterm Examination :

Midterm Examinations are held on the 8 th week of Fall/Spring semester according to the timetables announced by the Student Affairs Office (sent by Email to students).

Final Examinations

Final Examinations will start at the 17 th week of Fall/Spring semester according to the timetables announced by the Student Affairs Office (sent by Email to students).

Precautions regarding Midterm and Final Examination Procedures

The following general rules must be followed:

- A student must place student ID on the desk with the photograph side up.

- A student must follow the directions of exam instructor/proctor.

- A student must NOT engage in any improper conduct.

- mobile phones, smartphones, smart watches, etc. are prohibited in the exam classrooms.

- A student must enter the Exam room at the start time of the exam (late arriving student will be prohibited from taking the exam).

- A student may leave the examination venue after at least a half of exam duration has elapsed and if permitted by the exam instructor/proctor.

Treatment of improper conduct

A student who has engaged in improper conduct will be subject to E-JUST Student Disciplinary Regulations.

Basic Academic Information about the Pharmacy Program

Degree

The Faculty of Pharmacy at E-JUST awards the degree of Bachelor of Pharmaceutical Sciences (PharmD)

Graduation

In order to graduate from E-JUST (PharmD), the student must:

- successfully pass 179 credit hours (over at least 5 academic years) with an average CGPA not less than 2.00

- Complete 100 hours of Summer field training in a suitable Pharmacy related facility (after completing the third level).

- Complete 6 rotations (6 weeks each) during the Internship Year at approved site(s).

Transfer between levels

A student will be transferred from level 1 to level 2 after successfully completing 36 credit hours, and from level 2 to level 3 after successfully completing 70 credit hours, from level 3 to level 4 after completing 108 credit hours, and from level 4 to level 5 after successfully completing 145 credit hours.
