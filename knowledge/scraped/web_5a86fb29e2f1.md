## Personal Info

I am an Associate Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at the Faculty of Engineering, Egypt-Japan University of Science and Technology. With a distinguished academic career, he earned his Bachelor's from Tanta University in 2004, graduating with honors. He subsequently completed his Master's degree in 2012 at the same institution. His Ph.D. was awarded in 2015 by the Egypt-Japan University of Science and Technology (E-JUST), focusing on applications of compressed sensing in multimedia.

My academic journey at Tanta University includes appointments as a Demonstrator in 2005, Assistant Lecturer in 2012, and Lecturer in 2015, culminating in his promotion to Associate Professor in 2021. Alongside his teaching and research, he is active in administrative and scientific roles within the faculty, enhancing the academic and operational framework of the department.

My research interests span signal processing, compressed sensing, and multimedia applications, And deep learning applications. He has contributed extensively to the field through numerous publications in esteemed journals and conferences. He is also known for his teaching and mentoring, supporting the development of future engineers in his department.
