Welcome to the Faculty of Art & Design (AnD), where our slogan ‘A Journey to Ultimate Creativity’ is more than a statement — it is a philosophy that shapes every stage of our students’ academic and professional development.

At our AnD, creativity is not left to chance. It is cultivated through structured exploration, critical thinking, experimentation, and real-world engagement. We believe that true creativity reaches its ultimate form when it is supported by knowledge, responsibility, and strategic vision. That is why we uniquely equip our students with a solid foundation in Intellectual Property, ensuring they understand how to protect, manage, and commercialise their ideas in today’s competitive global market.

Our commitment to experiential learning is reflected in providing two structured work placements for every student during their academic journey. These placements bridge theory and practice, expose students to professional environments, and strengthen their readiness for the evolving design industry.

We focus on building strong designer mindsets — analytical, innovative, and solution-oriented. Beyond developing designers, we transform our students into ‘Design-Preneurs’ creative professionals who combine design excellence with entrepreneurial thinking, market awareness, and strategic planning.

Most importantly, we strive to graduate proactive, self-motivated, and resilient design leaders who are prepared to thrive in the future global design job market. Our graduates do not simply seek opportunities — they create them.

This is our journey…

A journey toward excellence…

A journey toward impact…

A journey to Ultimate Creativity!

Dr. Ahmed Romouzy
