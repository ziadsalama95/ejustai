## Guidelines and Application Procedures

E-JUST TICAD9 African Postdoctoral Fellowship for STI — Fall 2027

This fellowship supports qualified African researchers undertaking advanced postdoctoral research at E-JUST, while strengthening research collaboration and sustainable STI networks connecting African, Egyptian, and Japanese institutions.

Fellowship duration: 9 to 12 months | Available slots: 10

## Fellowship Overview

### E-JUST TICAD9 African Postdoctoral Fellowship for STI Guidelines and Application Procedures for Fall 2027

## Objective of the Program

The overall objective of the Postdoctoral Fellowship Program is to contribute to the development of highly qualified human resources in science, technology, and innovation (STI) and to strengthen the foundation for a sustainable African STI network connecting E-JUST, African universities and research institutions, and Japanese universities and research institutions.

Through this program, E-JUST and JICA seek to establish a continuing pathway from graduate education to advanced research and to strengthen a growing community of African researchers connected through E-JUST and its partner institutions in Japan and Africa.

The fellowship is designed to support research activities that are aligned with E-JUST's academic and research strengths and that have potential to contribute to scientific collaboration, capacity building, and the development of sustainable research networks between E-JUST and African institutions.

### The program aims to:

provide qualified African researchers with opportunities to undertake advanced postdoctoral research at E-JUST;

strengthen the research skills, expertise, and international experience of participating researchers;

promote joint and collaborative research between E-JUST and African and Japanese universities and research institutions;

strengthen academic and research networks among researchers and institutions in Africa, Egypt, and Japan;

facilitate the transfer of knowledge, research skills, and technological expertise to African universities and research institutions; and

contribute to the long-term development of STI capacity in Africa.

## Scope of the Fellowship

The Postdoctoral Fellowship Program provides selected researchers with an opportunity to conduct postdoctoral research at E-JUST under the academic supervision of an eligible E-JUST faculty member or research supervisor.

### The fellowship may include:

conducting an approved postdoctoral research project;

accessing relevant laboratories, research facilities, equipment, and academic resources at E-JUST;

participating in seminars, workshops, conferences, and other academic activities;

collaborating with E-JUST researchers and research groups;

developing research collaboration with African and Japanese partner institutions, where applicable;

preparing and submitting research papers to peer-reviewed journals and conference E-JUST listed as an affiliation; and

developing future research proposals and collaborative research activities.

The specific research activities shall be determined in consultation with the E-JUST host department, school and supervisor and shall be subject to the availability of appropriate research facilities and resources.

## Target Applicants

- Postdoctoral opportunities are offered to African researchers who earned PhD and/or M.Sc. degree preferably in E-JUST.

## Qualifications

The Postdoctoral Fellowship Program is intended for qualified researchers from African countries, excluding Egypt.

### Applicants should normally meet the following requirements:

Hold a Ph.D. degree in a field relevant to the proposed postdoctoral research.

Be working as a researcher, academic instructor, or university faculty member at an African university or research institution, or be expected to undertake such a role.

Preferably have obtained a Ph.D. and/or M.Sc. degree from E-JUST.

Have an appropriate academic and research background relevant to the proposed research plan.

Demonstrate sufficient academic and research capability to undertake the proposed research at E-JUST.

Where applicable, have institutional support from the applicant's home university or research institution.

Intend, preferably, to continue their job (academic or research career) at their home institution following completion of the postdoctoral fellowship at E-JUST; endorsement of application forms by their home institutions is required in order to assure they would return to home institutions in the future

Not be serving in the military.

Pass the administrative, academic screening and the interviews.

Meet any additional eligibility requirements specified by E-JUST and/or JICA.

- Excellent command of written and spoken English, as English is the official language of instruction and communication at E-JUST.

Meeting the eligibility requirements does not in itself guarantee selection. Applications will be assessed through the applicable academic and administrative selection procedures.

## Selection Process

Applications shall undergo an academic and administrative review process in accordance with E-JUST and JICA requirements.

### The selection process may include the following stages:

- Eligibility Screening

Applications will be reviewed to determine whether applicants meet the basic eligibility requirements and whether all required documents have been submitted.

- Academic and Research Evaluation

Eligible applications will be evaluated based on relevant academic and research criteria, which may include academic qualifications; research experience and achievements; quality and relevance of the proposed research; feasibility of the research plan; relevance of the research to the host department and available facilities; qualifications and suitability of the proposed E-JUST supervisor; potential for research collaboration and potential contribution to the applicant's home institution;

- Interview

Shortlisted applicants will be invited for an interview conducted online by the academic department. The interview must address the applicant's academic background, research proposal, motivation, research plans, and future academic or professional plans.

- Final Approval

Final selection shall be subject to approval by the competent authorities at E-JUST (the council of Regional and International Affairs and University Council) and the availability of the fund.

### Energy Materials (EMA) program

- This track focuses on designing topological photonic crystal structures that enable robust wave manipulation and ultra-sensitive sensing through defect-immune edge, corner, and resonant states.

- This track explores rainbow trapping and slow-light phenomena in topological photonic systems to achieve broadband, spatially controlled, and disorder-robust light localization for advanced photonic devices.

- This track focuses on integrating topological photonic crystals with plasmonic nanostructures to realize robust, ultra-compact, and highly enhanced optical devices for sensing, energy, and active nanophotonics.

- This track investigates topological and rainbow-trapping-based nanolasers and active photonic devices with low thresholds, multi-wavelength operation, and robust performance against structural imperfections.

- Topological Photonics for Energy and Thermal Applications

- This track aims to develop topological photonic structures for efficient light trapping, thermal management, and energy conversion, enabling robust and angle-insensitive platforms for photovoltaic, thermophotovoltaic, and radiative thermal systems.
