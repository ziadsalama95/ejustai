# Help Desk and Enquiry

Help Desk

Help Desk services are critical to our library that provide information and support to remote users, providing a central resource for answering queries, troubleshooting issues, and facilitating solutions to known problems.

In addition, the library has provided electronic help desk tools to provide support for remote users. The librarian's email address, library@ejust.edu.eg
