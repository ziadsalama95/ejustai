Mr. Gehad Mongy

Secertary General gehad.mongy@ejust.edu.eg

At the Egypt-Japan University of Science and Technology (E-JUST), we believe that world-class education must be supported by world-class administration. As Secretary General, I am honored to lead an administrative system built on transparency, technological advancement, and a commitment to service excellence. Our approach is rooted in smart governance—utilizing cutting-edge digital tools, streamlined workflows, and data-driven decision-making to create efficient and responsive operations. We aim to provide a seamless experience for faculty, staff, and students while fostering a positive, healthy work environment that values employee well-being and supports a sustainable work-life balance. E-JUST’s administrative strategy is fully aligned with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs). From environmentally conscious practices to inclusive management policies, we strive to ensure that our operations contribute meaningfully to a sustainable future—on campus and beyond. As we move forward, we remain committed to continuous improvement, institutional resilience, and supporting the university’s broader mission of academic and research excellence.

Behind every great university is a foundation of well-coordinated, forward-thinking operations. At E-JUST, our commitment to excellence extends beyond classrooms and laboratories—it lives in every corner of our campus. As Deputy Secretary General for Administrative and Technical Affairs, my role is to ensure that our infrastructure, logistics, and technical services are not only efficient but future-ready. We invest in smart facility management systems, energy-efficient solutions, and digital infrastructure to maintain a safe, reliable, and sustainable campus environment. Our teams work diligently to support the daily life of the university—ensuring that buildings operate smoothly, technologies are up to date, and our spaces remain welcoming and functional for learning, research, and innovation. In line with our commitment to the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), we implement environmentally responsible practices across all technical and administrative functions—from resource optimization to green campus initiatives. We take pride in enabling a campus where people thrive, ideas grow, and every operation reflects the values of innovation, responsibility, and excellence.
