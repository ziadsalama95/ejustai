- Starting date: Fall

- Registration starts: May

- Duration: 3 years (6 semesters)

- Location: Alexandria (Borg Al-Arab) and Cairo (Smart Village)

- Fees: 165,000 EGP (55,000 per Year)

- Application fees: 1000 EGP (nonrefundable)

- Partial Academic scholarships (Discounts Up to 40%)

The Doctorate of Business Administration (DBA) is a doctorate program designed to provide candidates with advanced knowledge and skills in business administration. It is designed for experienced professionals who are seeking to enhance their knowledge, skills, and capabilities in the field of business administration. The program focuses on research, theory, and practical applications in various fields of business administration. The program aims to develop candidates' critical thinking, problem-solving, and decision-making skills necessary to excel in the dynamic and rapidly evolving business world. Our unique program grants a DBA certificate that is accredited by the Supreme Council of Universities . The curriculums are carefully prepared to follow the Japanese learning system while maintaining international standards to strengthen managerial and leadership skills.

- Minimum three years (maximum 6 years) The program spreads over three academic years, as a minimum, distributed as follows:

- Two years of coursework (36 credit hours)

- One year for Dissertation/thesis (24 credit hours).
