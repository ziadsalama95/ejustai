# Program Overview

Explore cutting edge science with the Molecular Biology and Biomedical Sciences Program, where curiosity meets innovation. Combining the principles of microbiology and genetics with advanced biomedical research, this program provides an in-depth study of life at the molecular level. Students acquire practical skills and hands-on experience that boost biotechnology, medicine, and healthcare breakthroughs.Prepare to lead breakthroughs that improve lives and shape the future of science.

## Program Mission

The MBB program is built on the belief that real learning happens through connection—across disciplines, campuses, and communities. We encourage our students to explore beyond the classroom through internships, independent projects, and hands-on research alongside dedicated faculty. This rich, collaborative environment helps them understand real-world challenges and contributes to both local and global progress.

Our goal is to give students a strong foundation in biomedical sciences while inspiring them to dive into the exciting world of life sciences research. We aim to meet the growing need for skilled professionals in this field, raise awareness about the impact of biotechnology, and build meaningful partnerships with industry. From ensuring the safe use of genetic technologies to harnessing the power of bioinformatics and computational biology, our program helps students become part of a connected, forward-thinking scientific community—both here and around the world.
