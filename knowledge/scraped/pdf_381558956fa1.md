E-JUST Campus and New Western Dorms Wall 
Branding 
Design workshop / public art / campus identity 
DATE / TIME 
April 2025 · day/time not specified 
LOCATION 
E-JUST Main Campus and new Western Dorms 
An intensive collaborative workshop developed wall-branding concepts, the university’s first mascot design (described as being registered 
as an E-JUST trademark), and a mosaic mural for the new Western Dorms. Designs aimed to improve campus visual identity, appeal, 
functionality, and navigation. 
Guided by Associate Prof. Dr. Ahmed Romouzy (Acting Dean), faculty academics, and teaching assistants. Work included student–faculty 
brainstorming and iterative feedback from the dean, academics, and teaching assistants; key outputs included wall branding, mascot and mosaic 
designs and concepts for campus and dormitory branding. 
Creative Industry Companies — Practical Training 
MoU Signing 
Industry partnership / design education 
DATE / TIME 
May 2025 · day/time not specified 
LOCATION 
E-JUST campus (exact room not specified) 
E-JUST welcomed representatives of creative and design companies to sign a memorandum of understanding with the Faculty of Art & 
Design. The partnership aims to provide practical student training and link university instruction with professional practice. Prof. Amr 
Adly, E-JUST President, opened the event and emphasized hands-on experience and academic–industry links. 
Participating organizations named: ElAraby Group; 3Brothers; Machines & Aluminum Center; Robota Industries; Pyramakerz Technologies; 
Omran Architects; Heads Design and Build L.L.C.; Archvision Consultancy; Insight Design; Perry Touch Office; Creative Advertising; PL Events; 
Metas Research Studio; and E-JUST Public Relations & Projects Management Unit. Attendees included Prof. Sameh Nada (Vice President for 
Education and Academic Affairs), university deans, Ms. Noha Afifi (Director, Center for Innovation and Technology), and Dr. Tsunoda Manabu 
(Chief Advisor, JICA Office at E-JUST). Associate Prof. Ahmed Romouzy (Acting Dean) presented the Faculty; an open collaboration discussion 
and a campus tour visited the H.E. Fayza Abouelnaga Main Library and sports facilities. Four programs covered: Industrial Product Design, 
Interior Design, Digital Media Design, and Arts in Creative Industries.

Hiroshima University Winter Program — 
Collaborative Workshop 
International workshop / cross-cultural learning 
DATE / TIME 
13 February 2026 (Friday) 
LOCATION 
E-JUST (specific room not specified) 
A Hiroshima University–E-JUST collaborative workshop brought together 60 participants (30 Japanese and 30 Egyptian students). 
Activities used the Six Thinking Hats method to structure dialogue, problem-solving, perspectives, teamwork, active participation, and 
intercultural learning. 
Under the supervision of Associate Professor Dr. Ahmed Romouzy, Acting Dean of the Faculty of Art & Design. Art & Design students and 
teaching assistants Hager Elsayed and Mohamed Reda helped facilitate activities and discussions. The program fostered connections among future 
designers, researchers and professionals.

Tsukuba University Summer Program / Japan–Africa 
Youth Exchange 
Cultural exchange / academic visits 
DATE / TIME 
August 2026 · two-day program; exact dates not specified 
LOCATION 
E-JUST and Cairo cultural sites 
E-JUST hosted Japanese participants and E-JUST students from Egypt, Palestine and African countries for a two-day Japan–Africa Youth 
Exchange Program focused on dialogue, cultural exchange and shared learning. As part of E-JUST’s special summer program for the 
University of Tsukuba, students explored Cairo’s architecture, traditions and cultural history. 
Visits included the Grand Egyptian Museum, Pyramids, Salah El-Din Citadel, Mohamed Ali Mosque and Baron Palace. The visits encouraged 
cross-cultural understanding and connections among participants.
