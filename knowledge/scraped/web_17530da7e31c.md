# Welcome to E-JUST Library

### Welcome

We welcome you to Fayza Aboulnaga Central Library (FACL), the heart of knowledge and research at Egypt-Japan University of Science and Technology (E-JUST). We are delighted to welcome you, whether you are here to read, conduct research, ignite your creativity, or simply enjoy a quiet place to study. Whether you are working individually or as part of a group, engaging in a study session, presentation, or project discussions. We are committed to providing a welcoming and enriching environment for all, our library is here to support your needs. With a vast collection of resources and librarians ready to assist with your inquiries, we aim to provide everything you need for a productive visit.

Our library goes beyond just a repository of books; it is a vibrant center of learning and discovery, specifically designed to support the scientific excellence and innovation that characterizes E-JUST. Feel free to participate in our events and workshops, and if you need any assistance, do not hesitate to approach our staff. Our dedicated library staff is always ready to provide support and assistance. Take advantage of our various services, including lending, catalog searching, access to digital resources, and assistance in developing your research skills. We hope you find our library a source of inspiration and growth.

We hope you find the Fayza Aboulnaga Central Library a peaceful haven and a valuable resource that will help you achieve your academic and personal goals. We look forward to your visit and serving you!
