- How to Apply Guide

- Starting date: Fall

- Registration starts: May

- Duration: 2 years (4 semesters)

- Location: Alexandria (Borg Al-Arab) and Cairo (Smart Village)

- Fees: 80,000 EGP (20,000 per semester)

- Application fees: 1000 EGP (nonrefundable)

- Partial Academic scholarships (Discounts Up to 40%)

In the current competitive landscape, holding a professional degree like the E-JUST MBA will enable you to take your professional career to the next level. The Faculty of International Business and Humanities at E-JUST offers a two-year, part-time MBA program with five different tracks to accommodate your needs. Our unique program grants an MBA certificate that is accredited by the Supreme Council of Universities . The curriculums are carefully prepared to follow the Japanese learning system while maintaining international standards to strengthen managerial and leadership skills.

During the first year, all students study the same courses (Core Courses) to build a firm understanding of how businesses operate in the real world. In the second year, we give you the opportunity to choose the track that will best allow you to achieve your personal goals. Each track consists of three compulsory courses (9 credit hours), an applied research project (6 credit hours), and three elective courses (9 credit hours). The following five tracks are available:

- Master of Business Administration (MBA) General

- Master of Business Administration in Accounting (MBA-ACC)

- Master of Business Administration in Finance (MBA-FIN)

- Master of Business Administration in Marketing Management and Digital

- Master of Business Administration in Human Resource Management (MBA-HRM)
