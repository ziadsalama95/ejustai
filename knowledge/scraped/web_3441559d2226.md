- E-JUST provides its Ph.D. students (Egyptians and International) with opportunities to study in Japan and spend a period of 6 – 9 months in one of E-JUST’s cooperation universities to conduct their research studies.

- Egypt Japan Education Partnership (EJEP) is an educational partnership between Egyptian government and Japanese government to build a peaceful and prosperous society. There are opportunities for E-JUST students to apply for several programs in Japan through EJEP, such as semester / 1 year study in Japanese universities, summer program, etc.

- In addition to cooperation with various universities, some universities offer even more support. They provide exchange programs (a maximum of 2 semesters) that exempt students from tuition fees and help students to find suitable accommodation.

Below is a list of universities that provide such support:

- Kyushu University

- Tohoku University

- Saitama University

- Jomo Kenyatta University of Agriculture and Technology

- Ritsumeikan University
