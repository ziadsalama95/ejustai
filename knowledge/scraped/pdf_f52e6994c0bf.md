Sakura Science Exchange Program-  May 2023
Upon our visit to Japan we met with Prof. Hayashi from Tokyo Tech who presented 
an excellent proposition of an Entrepreneurship Education program. 
This program can be implemented at E-JUST and can enhance collaboration with 
both Egyptian and Japanese companies.
 The program is built on fortifying the role of liberal arts in enhancing collaboration 
between specializations and entrepreneurship. 
Through fusing specialization, entrepreneurship, and liberal arts students may gain 
access to a new prospect for their careers. 
This will focus on strengthening their problem solving skills as well as various soft 
skills through liberal arts education. This proposition also includes contact with 
different industries to offers opportunities to students whereby hands on experience 
and practical application will be the essence of the program.

## Page 36

Japanese Language intensive summer course 
As a result of the Japanese Language intensive summer course , 25 students are ready to Join 
the JLPT Exam on December 2023,
The Japanese-Language Proficiency Test (日本語能力試験) or JLPT, is a test to evaluate and 
certify Japanese language proficiency for non-native speakers, covering language knowledge, 
reading ability, and listening ability.
The test is held twice a year on the first Sunday of July and December at the Japan Fpindation 
Headquarter in Cairo.
Coordination is now underway with the Japan Foundation to host the Test at EJUST Main 
Campus
