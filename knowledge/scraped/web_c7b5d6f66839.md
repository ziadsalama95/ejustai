## Personal Info

Tamer Fawzy Megahed received the B.Sc., M.Sc., and Ph.D. degrees in electrical engineering, in 2006, 2010, and 2015, respectively. In 2016, He was appointed as an Assistance Professor in the Electrical Engineering Department at the Faculty of Engineering, Mansoura University. Currently, He is a Professor and Chairman of the Electrical Engineering Department at the Egypt-Japan University of Science and Technology (E-JUST).

He got many funded projects and now he owns four projects funded by STDS and JICA in the following research points to improve the performance of wind energy using magnetic technology, the use of IoT to monitor the load demand in housing and air-condition load analysis, and a multi-generational system to generate electricity, cooling, and fresh water. The total funding project is about 6000,000 USD.

Dr. Megahed has authored and co-authored more than 80 international publications, five books, and four patents in areas including magnetic refrigeration, IoT-based electricity monitoring systems, thrust-vector-control model rocket design, and magnetic gear technology. He is a member of IEEE, including the IEEE Power Electronics Society (PELS) and IEEE Industrial Electronics Society (IES), and serves as an editor for the journals Smart Grid and Sustainable Energy and Scientific Reports .

Research interests include power electronics control, renewable energy systems, smart grids, electric vehicles, wireless power transfer, energy storage systems, vector control of electrical machines, power system protection, model predictive control, and green hydrogen technologies.
