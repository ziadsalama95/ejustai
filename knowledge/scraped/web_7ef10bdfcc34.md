Egypt is one of the best places to live in the MENA region. It is renowned for its rich history and wonderful weather. Egyptians are known for their hospitality and friendliness. The main spoken language is Arabic and a lot of people can communicate in English as well.

Egypt is well known with its vibrant cuisine and unique touristic destination.

Alexandria is the second largest city in Egypt and a well-known hub for culture and history. It is located in the north of Egypt on the Mediterranean Sea coast. The city was found by the Alexander the great and was the capital of Egypt for over 1000 years until the Muslim conquest of Egypt.

Today, the city is a top destination to anyone visiting Egypt. No matter what your taste is and what is your budget there is something for everyone. There are several activities you can engage in while living there, you can enjoy the city’s long history by visiting historical sites and museums such as Qaitbay Citadel, , Alexandria National Museum, Roman Amphitheatre, Bibliotheca Alexandrina, Catacombs of Kom El Shoqafa and Jewelry Museum among other destinations. Borg Alrab is an new city that is designed with a lot of green spaces, it is a hot spot for several industries, research and innovation. Testimonial from Ms. Miayke

“What kind of image do you have of the word “Egypt”? You may think of ancient ruins such as Pyramids, Sphinx, Tutankhamun or great nature such as River Nile, huge desert and beautiful ocean. Egypt is a country which has both ancient romance and modern life.

There is no difficulty to be a part of the society as Egyptians are very friendly and have a strong spirit of hospitality. You can explore each area by using flight, train, bus, taxi, or tuktuk without any fear to be a foreigner and if you need any help, they do help you. You just need to take a minimum safety measure like when you are in your home country and take one step forward to enjoy exploring streets and unique foods. E-JUST is welcoming you to be a part of E-JUST family.”
