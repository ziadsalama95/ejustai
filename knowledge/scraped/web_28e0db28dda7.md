## Departments

Discover the specialized academic departments within the Faculty of Pharmacy, each contributing to an integrated pharmaceutical education through advanced teaching, research, practical training, and scientific excellence.

### Molecular Therapeutics

Contains 3 Academic departments: Biochemistry & Molecular Biology (PBM), Clinical Pharmacy & Pharmacy Practice (PCP), Pharmacology & Toxicology (PCT).

### Pharmaceutical Chemistry

Contains 2 Academic departments: Pharmaceutical Analytical Chemistry (PAC) and Medicinal Chemistry (PMC).

### Natural Products

Contains 2 Academic departments: Microbiology & Immunology (PMI) and Pharmacognosy (PHG).

### Pharmaceutics

Contains Academic department Pharmaceutics & Industrial Pharmacy (PIP).
