E-JUST VISION:   
To be a world class university in science and technology based on best Japanese education 
practices.   
ARTICLE-2   
E-JUST MISSION:   
 To become a role model university for undergraduate and graduate education, 
scientific research and innovation in Egypt by nurturing the Japanese educational 
standards, policies, and systems.   
 To converge all the needed efforts to make the EJUST academic degrees recognized 
internationally.   
 To prepare qualified and trained human resources to meet the needs of the local, 
regional and international labor market through the provision of distinguished 
academic and professional programs. 
   To promote and support the establishment of entrepreneurship, technical and 
commercializes between Japanese industries and organizations, and their 
counterparts in countries and regions which are served by E-JUST.   
ARTICLE 3   
E-JUST OBJECTIVES:  
 To strength the role of the university in the production and transfer of knowledge and 
skills to serve the community and achieve the highest standards of academic 
excellence.

ARTICLE 9  
ACADEMIC SEMESTERS AND REGISTRATION  
1. The academic year is divided into three semesters:  
• 
The fall semester starts at the beginning of the fourth week of September and 
continues for 15 weeks, excluding the mid-term and final examinations.  
• 
The spring semester starts at the beginning of the third week of February and 
continues for 15 weeks, excluding the mid-term and final examinations.  
• 
The summer semester, which is a condensed semester, starts at the beginning 
of the first week of July and continues for 6 weeks, excluding the mid-term and 
final examinations.

ARTICLE 10 
GENERAL ADMISSION REQUIREMENTS  
1. To register for a M. Sc. degree in one of the programs cited in Article-7, the student must 
possess a bachelor degree in engineering or in engineering and technology with a 
CGPA of at least 2.5 (on GPA-scale of 4) or an equivalent cumulative percentage of at 
least 75%. The student BSc degree should be obtained from an Egyptian university or 
any other academic institution (in Egypt or abroad) recognized by the Supreme Council 
of Universities (SCU) in Egypt.   
2. To register for a Ph. D. degree in one of the programs cited in Article-7, the student 
must possess a bachelor degree in engineering or in engineering and technology and a 
M.Sc. degree in Engineering. His BSc and MSc degrees should be obtained from an 
Egyptian university or any other academic institution (in Egypt or abroad) recognized by 
the Supreme Council of Universities (SCU) in Egypt.  
3. The student should pass successfully all the entrance examinations, interview and 
evaluation.  
4. Student should have TOFEL iBT score of at least 79 or Academic IELTS 6.5 for 
admission. Egyptian Students with TOFEL iBT score less than 79 but greater than or 
equal to 65 or Academic IELTS less than 6.5 but greater than or equal to 5.5 can be 
provisionally accepted. Those provisionally accepted students must register for the 
English language course offered within the preparatory courses and should satisfy the 
English language requirements (TOFEL iBT score of at least 79 or Academic IELTS 6.5) 
within the first three semesters from his/her admission; otherwise the student enrollment 
will be terminated.  
5. The student should complete and submit to the registration office, all the required 
documents determined by the Education and Academic Affairs Council.  
6. The student should satisfy all other requirements determined by the respective School 
Council and approved by the university council.  
7. The student is required to pay the predetermined tuition fees approved by the university 
council or should be supported by scholarships or a grant.

ARTICLE 11 
TUITION FEES   
1. The University Council determines the tuition fees early March annually.   
2. The student pays the tuition fees at the beginning of the fall and spring semesters.   
3. The student enrolled in a program and intended to withdraw from the program, 
cannot regain the tuition fees that he has paid.   
4. In case the student requests a study extension period, and his request is approved 
by the university council, he/she should pay the tuition fees at the beginning of this 
period.   
ARTICLE 12 
ACADEMIC ADVISOR  
The respective Department Council assigns an academic advisor of the rank of professor or 
associate professor, for each graduate student. The academic advisor will be responsible 
for:  
• 
Advising the student during his course work 
• 
Helping the student to select the preparatory courses according to his statues and 
qualifications.   
• 
Helping the student to select the elective courses relevant to the field he wishes to 
study.   
• 
Recommending to the related Department Council (for its approval) the preparatory 
courses and any other additional complementary courses that, in the opinion of the 
academic advisor, the student has to take.  
ARTICLE 13 
STUDY SYSTEM   
1. The study in the graduate programs is in credit hours system. The regulations and 
requirements are indicated in Articles -14 through -21. The total number of credit hours 
is 36 credit hours for the MSc program, and 48 credit hours for the PhD program. The 
credits hours for the MSc and PhD programs are distributed between the courses and 
thesis as follows:  
 21 credit hours for courses and 15 credit hours for thesis for the MSc program. 
 21 credit hours for courses and 27 credit hours for thesis for the PhD program.

2. The 21 credit hours of the MSc and PhD programs courses are divided as follows: 
 Preparatory courses (six courses; four university requirements courses and two 
department requirements courses; each course of 1 credit hour)  
 Program compulsory core courses (two courses; each of 3 credit hours)  
 Research specific elective courses (three courses including a project based learning 
course for MSc students and seminar course for PhD students; each course of 3 credit 
hours) 
3. The students cannot register the research elective courses before successfully finishing 
12 credit hours of the preparatory and the program compulsory core courses. Student 
who get exempted from any of the program compulsory core courses can register either 
project based course for MSc students or seminar course for PhD students. 
  
4. The research point, research plan and the supervision committee of the student are 
recommended the department council and approved by the school council and the 
educational and academic affairs councils after the student successfully completes the 
preparatory courses and fulfills the English language requirements..

 Courses of 700-Level for project and/or seminar courses for MSC and PhD 
students.

during the summer semester.

to do hi/hers MSc study in another relevant specialization may be asked to pass 
successfully complementary courses from the undergraduate courses list, as 
recommended by the academic advisor through the Department Council and

ARTICLE 15 
PREPARATORY COURSES   
1. The objectives of the preparatory courses are: 
 To cover the deficiencies of E-JUST PG applicants in the basic research skills, 
English languages, computer programming, statistics and liberal arts. 
 To prepare E-JUST PG students to the required nature of PG study in E-JUST 
including Japanese culture, Japanese language, Arabic language for international 
students, Research and Publications ethic and method.   
 To make the PG students familiar with E-JUST labs, center of excellences and 
professors before registering the point of research and before the formation of the 
supervision committee. This will help the student in the proper selection of the 
research point and supervision committee.   
The preparatory courses are divided into university requirements courses (4 course; 
each 1 credit hour) and department elective courses (2 course; each 1 credit hour) 
2. The student has to successfully finish the preparatory courses before the registration 
of the point of research and the formation of the supervision committee.

ARTICLE 17 
MECHANISM OF EXEMPTING PREPARATORY COURSES   
 In consultation with the academic advisor, the student shall submit a request of waving 
preparatory course(s) as per the rule stated in article 16 with the supporting documents 
such as relevant certificates to the university. 
 The concerned committee (formed by VPE) in consultation with the concerned 
department/school will examine and approve course waving requests.  
 The VPE approves the committee recommendation in the education council. 
 
ARTICLE 18 
PROGRAM COMPULSORY CORE COURSES   
1. Each program has two core compulsory courses for the program; each course three 
hours credits. These courses are compulsory courses for the program.  
2. The student has to register the program compulsory core courses with the preparatory 
courses in the first year.  
3. In case the student registers PhD after getting MSc from E-Just, he/she will be 
exempted from the program compulsory core courses (6 credit hours) if the PhD and 
MSc subjects are in the same specialization and track. 
4. Upon a written evidence from the student academic advisor, the Ph. D. student who 
exempted from the program compulsory courses (500 level courses) is exempted from 
one or two department 400 level preparatory courses.   
5. Ph. D. students got their M. Sc. from E-JUST are automatically exempted from the 
program compulsory courses (the two 500 level courses) and the English PY course.

ARTICLE 19 
PROGRAM RESEARCH ELECTIVE COURSES   
1. Each program has three courses related to the research topics; each course is three 
credit hours.

5. One of these three courses can be selected from other graduate program. 
6. The three courses should be selected related to the student research track according 
to the recommendations of the main supervisor.

THESIS EXAMINATION COMMITTEE AND PROCEDURE  
1. Before appointing the thesis examination committee, the M.Sc. or PhD. student should 
conduct a public seminar on his/her thesis research results and findings.  
2. To form the thesis examination committee, the main supervisor submits the following 
documents to the respective Department Council:  
• 
A report on the legibility of the thesis for defense, including the achieved 
publications.  
• 
A proposal for the thesis examination committee.  
• 
A draft of the thesis.  
3. Upon the request of the main supervisor and a recommendation from the Department 
Council, the Education and Academic Affairs Council appoints a committee to judge 
and publicly discuss the thesis.  
4. 
The examination committee consists of no more than two supervisors; one of 
them is the main supervisor, both accounting for a single vote, and two other members; 
at least one of them should be from outside E-JUST (External) and must be of the rank 
of at least professor or PhD-holder industrial expert matching at least associate 
professor level, with suitable publications record. The thesis examination committee is 
headed by the most senior member of the committee.  
5. The examiners should have adequate academic experience and recent publication(s) 
in the field of the thesis.

E-JUST Graduate Bylaws, Sep. 2018

6. If the individual reports from the Thesis examination committee members are positive 
and in favor of the Thesis, the following actions are taken:  
a. The Department Council sets a date for the Thesis defense based on the 
request of the main supervisor.  
b. The Thesis defense is carried out in the set date with the presence of all 
members of Thesis examination committee. If two members from the 
supervision committee are nominated in the Thesis examination committee, the 
Thesis defense can be held in the presence of only one of them.  
7. After holding the Thesis examination, the Thesis examination committee submits to 
the Department Council a collective report on the Thesis. If the collective report comes 
in favor of the Thesis, it should include a statement indicating that the Thesis 
examination committee recommends granting the student the M.Sc or PhD. degree in 
the field of specialization. The Thesis evaluation has no grade; only it’s granting will be 
pointed out in the final certificate.  
8. The Thesis examination committee may recommend that the Thesis be returned to the 
student to fix whatever deficiency it contains. In case of a major deficiency discovered 
in the Thesis, the examination committee may grant a period of no more than three 
months to the student to finish what he was asked to do. At the end of that period, the 
student defends the Thesis before the same Thesis examination committee.   
9. If the Thesis examination committee rejects the Thesis, the collective report must 
contain the reasons for such a recommendation.  
10. Based on the recommendations of the Thesis examination committee and the 
Department Council, the Education and Academic Affairs Council recommends to the 
University Council granting the student the M.Sc. or PhD degree in the field of 
specialization, or terminating his/her enrollment.
