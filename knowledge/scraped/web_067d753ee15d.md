Focuses on space science, satellite data interpretation, and remote sensing technologies for monitoring Earth's and space environments. The program equips students for careers in aerospace, meteorology, remote sensing and environmental analysis.

Providing qualified scientific graduates that contribute to meeting the national and international needs in the field of Space Environment and Remote Sensing and sustain the Egyptian Space Program.
