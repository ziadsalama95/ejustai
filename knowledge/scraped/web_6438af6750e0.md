The Gender Equality and Counselling Unit (GEU) aims to achieve the fifth goal of the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) 'Achieving Gender Equality and Women's Empowerment' by supporting the university community (both genders) in addition to providing services to the surrounding community in the future by holding awareness sessions, workshops, and presenting various success stories that help everyone to reach a better life.

The Psychological Counseling Task, provides confidential psychological support to students and university staff. The task helps individuals cope with stress, anxiety, and personal difficulties in a supportive and safe environment. Through its counseling services, it promotes mental well-being, emotional resilience, and a healthy, inclusive university community where everyone feels supported and valued.
