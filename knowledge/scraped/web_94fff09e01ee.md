### E-JUST to Host “E-JUST Connect – JBA Special Session” on 8 April 2026

### Special session to advance industry–academia collaboration, technology transfer, and engagement with Japanese industry partners

The Egypt-Japan University of Science and Technology (E-JUST), through its Industry and Community Support Cluster (ICSC), will organize “E-JUST Connect – JBA Special Session” on Wednesday, 8 April 2026, from 10:00 AM to 2:00 PM, at the E-JUST Main Campus, New Borg El Arab, Alexandria, Egypt.

The event is being held as part of E-JUST’s continuing efforts to strengthen ties between academia and industry and to expand strategic engagement with Japanese industrial partners. The session will serve as a platform to highlight the University’s advanced research ecosystem, specialized laboratories, academic expertise, and the capabilities of its students and researchers.

E-JUST Connect – JBA Special Session is designed to support stronger industry-driven research collaboration , promote joint research, technology transfer, and innovation , and provide participants with direct exposure to E-JUST’s advanced facilities and interdisciplinary strengths. The event also reflects the University’s commitment to supporting the broader Japan–Egypt strategic partnership through impactful collaboration in higher education, scientific research, and industrial development.

Through this initiative, E-JUST seeks to create new pathways for consultation, knowledge exchange, and long-term cooperation with industry stakeholders, while further reinforcing its role as a bridge between academic excellence and practical industrial application.

The session is also expected to contribute to deeper dialogue on future opportunities for collaboration in research, training, innovation support, and the effective utilization of university expertise to address industrial challenges and foster sustainable development.

E-JUST welcomes its distinguished guests and partners to this special session and looks forward to a productive and meaningful gathering that will contribute to building enduring collaborative relationships and advancing shared objectives in research, innovation, and industry support.
