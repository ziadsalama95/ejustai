## BAS News

### Visit of the Egyptian-Japanese School of Borg El-Arab at 23/4/2026

In a collaborative outreach effort between the BAS Space Program and the E-JUST Space Club, a special visit was organized to Japanese School 2 in Borg El-Arab on April 23, 2026, aiming to inspire young minds through the wonders of space science. The event introduced students to fundamental concepts in astronomy through engaging, hands-on activities, including telescope demonstrations and interactive CanSat projects. The students showed remarkable enthusiasm and curiosity, actively participating and expressing genuine excitement about space exploration. This visit reflects a shared commitment to fostering scientific awareness and nurturing future talent in STEM fields. The organizing teams extend their sincere appreciation to the school’s principal and administration for their warm hospitality and support, and they look forward to continuing such impactful educational initiatives in the future.
