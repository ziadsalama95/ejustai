The E-JUST Summer Program offers international students a unique opportunity to immerse themselves in the rich culture of Egypt. Through interactive workshops, cultural activities, and Arabic language sessions, participants gain meaningful cross-cultural exposure. It is a wonderful opportunity to explore Egypt’s culture, language, and landmarks, accompanied by E-JUST students, experienced supervisors, and expert teachers.

Let's discover the wonders of the East while building lifelong friendships!

### Program Objectives:

- To deepen the understanding of Egyptian culture, Heritage and Society

- To learn Egyptian colloquial dialect and standard Arabic as a foreign language

- To exchange knowledge and network with peer students from different backgrounds, and build a good relationship with them

By joining the E-JUST Summer Program, students do not have to worry about the planning, where they are going to stay and such as everything will be prepared and ready for them to arrive!

participants will engage in a variety of interactive and educational experiences, including:

- Arabic Language Courses

- Heritage lectures and workshops

- Visiting attractive excursion sites, Cairo and Alexandria.

- Cultural Exchange
