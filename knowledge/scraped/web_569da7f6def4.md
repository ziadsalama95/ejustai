Admission Requirement Admission Requirement Applicants to the National Postgraduate Programs at Egypt-Japan University of Science and Technology (E-JUST) must fulfill the following requirements: Applicants must hold a relevant Bachelor’s degree (for M.Sc.) or Master’s degree (for Ph.D.) from a recognized institution, with a strong academic record in the related field. Candidates are required to submit a complete online application , including all supporting documents and selection of up to three research topics within one department, prioritized according to their interests and academic background. Applicants must successfully pass the primary screening process , conducted by the university, to evaluate submitted documents and eligibility for further assessment. Shortlisted candidates are required to pass a written entrance examination and personal/academic interview , which may include the presentation of a research proposal. Final admission is granted based on the overall evaluation of academic qualifications, entrance exam results, and interview performance . Admitted applicants must submit all original required documents within the specified deadline to complete the registration process. Applicants from governmental universities may be required to provide official approval from their affiliated institutions in accordance with scholarship or nomination regulations.
