## Welcome from the Dean

At the heart of every scientific breakthrough and technological leap lies a strong foundation in the basic sciences. At BAS, we believe that disciplines such as Chemistry, Mathematics, Physics, Space Science and Biomedical Sciences are not only cornerstones of knowledge but also catalysts for innovation, sustainability, transformative change and entrepreneurship.

Our undergraduate and postgraduate programs are designed to ignite curiosity, foster interdisciplinary research, and develop the next generation of scientists, innovators, and leaders. From pioneering studies in Applied Mathematics, Nanoscience and Energy Materials to advancing biomedical applications and theoretical frameworks, BAS students are equipped with the skills, mindset, and ethical grounding needed to tackle the most pressing challenges, locally and globally.

We invite passionate, creative, and driven students to join our dynamic educational and research environments, where scientific excellence meets real-world impact.

Prof. Ahmed Egiza

Acting as Dean,

School of Basic and Applied Sciences (BAS)
