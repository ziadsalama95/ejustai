International Business & Humanities Admissions Admission Requirements: | Program | Academic Background | | --- | --- | | Accounting and Information Systems (AIS) | The students’ academic background should be: ● To register for a M.Sc. student must hold a bachelor’s degree in accounting and information technology. ● To register for PhD must hold MSc degree in Accounting and Information Technology. | | Investment and Finance (FIN) | The students’ academic background should be: ● To register for a M.Sc. student must hold a bachelor’s degree in finance and Investment. ● To register for Ph.D., one must hold M.Sc. degree in Finance and Investment. | | Human Resource Management (HRM) | The students’ academic background should be: ● To register for a M.Sc. student must hold a bachelor’s degree in business administration ● To register for PhD must hold MSc degree in Business Administration. | | Management and Organizational Behavior (MGT) | The students’ academic background should be: ● To register for a M.Sc. student must hold a bachelor’s degree in business administration. ● To register for PhD must hold MSc degree in Business Administration. | | Marketing and International Business Studies (MKT) | The students’ academic background should be: ● To register for a M.Sc. student must hold a bachelor’s degree in marketing and international business. ● To register for PhD must hold MSc degree in Marketing and International Business. |
