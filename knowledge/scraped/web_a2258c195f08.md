# Program Overview

Chemistry offers comprehensive analytical, organic, inorganic, and physical chemistry knowledge. The program emphasizes practical laboratory skills and equips students to address real-world chemical challenges in industrial, energy, and environmental contexts. The mission of the Chemistry program is to provide a rigorous and comprehensive education in the chemical sciences, grounded in both theoretical knowledge and practical skills. The program aims to cultivate scientific literacy, critical thinking, and ethical responsibility in students, preparing them for successful careers in science, industry, education, or advanced study. Through innovative teaching, hands-on laboratory experience, and research opportunities, we seek to inspire curiosity, foster discovery, and contribute to solving real-world problems through the application of chemistry.

## Program Mission

Provide Applied Chemical Science-based graduates that meet different fields' needs and eventually become the main drivers for the sustainable development, and foster graduates to be creative, engage collaboratively, and be able to deal with local and regional communities.
