## Personal Info

Dr. Mostafa El-Sayed is an Associate Professor at EJUST University (Egypt-Japan University of Science and Technology, Faculty of Computer Science and Information Technology CSIT, since July 2025) and Minia University (Department of Computer Science, since April 2025). He has over 16 years of teaching experience and works.

High-performance computing, distributed systems, cloud computing, machine learning and deep learning, knowledge description and representation, ontology and the semantic web (OBDA), and Optimization
