# About Program

Nanoscience and Nanotechnology operate at the intersection of chemistry and materials science, this multidisciplinary program bridges the gap between molecular synthesis and macro-scale technology. Designed for ambitious researchers, our evolving curriculum transcends traditional boundaries to foster lateral thinking. Graduates emerge equipped with the critical research skills needed to drive innovation and address global challenges in sustainability, health, and industry.

## Program Vision

To be an internationally recognized center of excellence in Nanoscience and Nanotechnology, driving sustainable innovation in energy, healthcare, environment, and smart manufacturing.

## Program Mission

To equip MSc and PhD researchers with advanced skills in nanoscience and nanotechnology through an interdisciplinary, hands-on curriculum bridging chemistry, biochemistry, and materials science. Leveraging E-JUST’s advanced facilities and faculty mentorship, we cultivate entrepreneurial, ethical leaders ready to solve complex global challenges, launch deep-tech solutions, and drive innovation-driven economies.

## Partnerships & collaborations

Partnerships strengthen research capabilities, facilitate knowledge exchange, and provide students with exposure to international standards and technologies. The program maintains strong collaborations with several institutes
