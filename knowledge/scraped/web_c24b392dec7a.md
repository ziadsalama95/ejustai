### North Campus — Unified Model

Cost:

Room Contents:

Bed, comforter, pillow, wardrobe, desk, office chair, wall-mounted bookshelf, drawer unit, nightstand and bed linens.

- Laundry room on the ground floor, available all day.

- Five microwaves in the building, available all day.

- Three refrigerators on each floor in hotel-style buildings and one refrigerator in each apartment in apartment buildings.

- Viewing halls and study rooms in some buildings.

- All rooms are air-conditioned and have Wi-Fi access.

### West Campus

Four different room models are available.

- Model 1: Private bathroom and multiple areas — 4,500 EGP/month , 18,000 EGP/semester.

- Model 2: 22 m² — 3,800 EGP/month , 15,200 EGP/semester.

- Model 3: 14 m² — 3,500 EGP/month , 14,000 EGP/semester.

- Model 4: 12 m² — 3,000 EGP/month , 12,000 EGP/semester.

- Bed, comforter, pillow, wardrobe, desk, desk chair, hanging bookshelf, drawer unit, nightstand and bed linens.

- Laundry room on the ground floor, available all day.

- Each floor has a kitchen with refrigerator, microwave and water kettle.

- All rooms are air-conditioned and have Wi-Fi access.

### South Campus — Apartment Style

Each apartment consists of three rooms.

- Fully furnished with bed, comforter, pillow, wardrobe, desk, desk chair, hanging bookshelf, drawer unit, nightstand and bed linens.

- Laundry room on the ground floor, available all day.

- Five microwaves in the building, available all day.

- Refrigerator in each apartment for all residents.

- All rooms are air-conditioned and have Wi-Fi access.
