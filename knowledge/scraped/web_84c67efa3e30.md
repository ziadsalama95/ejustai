# Library Collection

- The library contains a wide and diverse collection in English, Arabic and Japanese languages including:

- Reference materials and textbooks relevant to the academic needs of EJUST's faculties, as well as resources on language learning, general subjects across various fields and novels.

- Postgraduate theses (MSc and PhD).

- Gift books donated by distinguished individuals, faculty members, Japanese universities, and organizations.

- You can search through the library’s collection via the OPAC .
