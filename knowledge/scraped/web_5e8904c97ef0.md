The E-JUST Faculty of Art & Design (AnD) embraces a distinctive and innovative approach to creativity, empowering students to address modern challenges with inspired, resourceful, and effective design solutions. We prepare future designers for a world rich with opportunities, equipping them to merge hands-on craftsmanship with imaginative thinking and adaptable methods across well-established disciplines—transforming ideas into reality.

Grounded in the principles of design thinking , our curriculum integrates cutting-edge digital tools with traditional techniques to deliver a learning experience that is both future-focused and deeply rooted in craftsmanship. Students are encouraged to adapt to the ongoing digital transformation , developing unique concepts from initial inspiration to final product while safeguarding their intellectual property rights .

Our programs are taught by a team of active academics and industry practitioners, offering expertise in:

- Industrial Product Design

- Interior Design

- Digital Media Design

- Arts in Creative Industries

Through this dynamic blend of theory, practice, and innovation, E-JUST prepares graduates to thrive as creative leaders in a rapidly evolving global landscape.

At E-JUST Art & Design , our students are designers, artists, innovators, and researchers who harness the transformative power of design with purpose, responsibility, and creativity. We cultivate a vibrant and diverse community that engages in meaningful activities—ranging from on-campus projects and research initiatives to collaborations that drive change locally, regionally, and globally.

We achieve this mission by building an agile academic environment that:

- Engages with contemporary global challenges through innovative and sustainable design practices.

- Centers on people and culture in shaping products, services, environments, and experiences that enhance human life.

- Places Project-Based Learning (PBL) at the core of the student journey, encouraging creativity through real-world problem solving.

- Leverages E-JUST’s strategic location to connect with leading global art and design institutions, fostering exchange and collaboration.

The E-JUST Faculty of Art & Design aspires to be a leading international hub for creativity, design excellence, and contemporary art. Our vision is to set the standard for multidisciplinary education, research, and practice—where critical and creative thinking, artistic expression, effective communication, cultural understanding, and community engagement come together in a dynamic global context.

Through regionally unique programs and a forward-looking approach, we aim to shape the future of art and design, inspiring graduates to become influential voices and innovators in their fields worldwide.

Studying at the Art & Design programs will develop:

- The ability to be creative

- The responsiveness to aesthetic

- The intellectual inquiry

- The teamwork skills

- The gratitude for the diversity

- The understanding of value and quality

- The capacity to explore and research in a different range of styles

- The merit of significantly reflecting on personal knowledge and progress

- The power to respect ethical considerations in a creative manner

- The ability to act autonomously, promoting resilience and self-determination

- The talent to connect in a range of designs.
