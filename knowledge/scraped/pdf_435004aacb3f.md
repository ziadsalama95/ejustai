Program Overview
E-JUST’s International Summer Program oﬀers international 
students a unique opportunity to explore Egypt’s rich 
culture, language, and heritage. Organized by the 
Regional and International Aﬀairs Oﬀice (RIA) in collaboration 
with the Liberal Arts and Culture Center (LACC). Participants 
will stay in E-JUST’s dormitory, engage with Egyptian 
students, and experience the life of an Egyptian student 
while building lifelong friendships, the program features:
• Interactive On-Campus lectures and Workshops
• Cultural Activities
• Excursions to Iconic Cities: Cairo and Alexandria
About E-JUST
Egypt-Japan University of Science and Technology 
(E-JUST) is a research university established in 2009 
through a partnership between Egypt and Japan. 
E-JUST is dedicated to creating a high-quality 
academic environment and setting a benchmark for 
education in Egypt and Africa. 
It exemplifies the strong collaboration between the 
two nations, working together toward lasting 
success.

FAQ:
Q: Do students have to be more than 18 years old to be eligible? 
A: Yes, we are currently targeting university students. However, accepting high school students might be 
possible in the upcoming programs.  
Q: Will there be a coordinator for the program?
A: Yes, an E-JUST staﬀ member will be in charge of coordinating the program and will be accompanying 
participants during the whole visit.
Q: What is included in the Summer program’s fees?
A: The program fees are ALL INCLUSIVE which covers accommodation and meals inside of E-JUST, 
hotels in 
visited cities, on campus activities, transportation within Egypt, the cost of tourist attractions tickets and 
meals during Alexandria and Cairo trips. 
Q: What is excluded from the Summer program’s fees?
A: The program fees do NOT include the flight tickets or any other personal expenses. 
Q: Do I need high English communication skills for the program?
A: Intermediate English communication skills are enough for daily conversations during your stay in 
Egypt. 
Q: Who is responsible for the visa application?
A: E-JUST will send the applicant an invitation letter afer his/her acceptance then the applicant shall 
apply for the visa using this letter. 
Q: Is there anyone who will pick the students from the airport?
A: An E-JUST staﬀ member and bus will be there to pick up the students from the airport. 
Q: In case of emergencies, will medical assistance be provided?
A: Yes, first aid is available at the E-JUST clinic on campus. In addition, the E-JUST program coordinator 
will accompany you to the hospital if needed.
Q: Do I receive a certificate of completion of the program?
A: A certificate of completion will be provided for the attendees at the farewell party on the last day of the 
program.
