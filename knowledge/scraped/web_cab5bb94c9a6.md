### Vision of the Unit

The main trigger for establishing and developing the college’s quality system so that it achieves national and international accreditation and continues to support its graduates to compete in the Egyptian, regional, and international job markets.

### The mission of the Unit

The unit is dedicated to fostering a culture of quality and ensuring the effective implementation of quality standards across all components of the educational institution. It manages and monitors the execution of the college’s strategic plan-including its educational, research, and community service activities-in alignment with the standards of the National Authority for Quality Assurance. The unit also develops an integrated quality assurance system to achieve national and international academic accreditation for the institution and its programs. Additionally, it is committed to continuous development and improvement to maintain and enhance the competitiveness of graduates in the Egyptian, regional, and international labor markets.

### Introduction

In its efforts to achieve national and international accreditation for its academic programs, the Egypt-Japan University of Science and Technology is committed to strengthening its quality assurance framework. In this context, the Faculty of Basic and Applied Sciences is working to build an integrated quality system at both the institutional and program levels, with the goal of applying for institutional and programmatic accreditation.

National accreditation is the formal recognition granted by the National Authority for Quality Assurance and Accreditation of Education (NAQAAE) to an educational institution or academic program ( https://naqaae.eg/ar/ ), once the faculty demonstrates adequate institutional capacity and educational effectiveness in accordance with the Authority’s approved and published standards, and shows that it has advanced systems capable of ensuring continuous improvement and enhancement of quality.

To support this process, the Quality Assurance Unit was established within the faculty to document and monitor continuous improvement across all academic and administrative systems and processes. Its mission is to ensure the production of high-quality, efficient outputs and to contribute to achieving the college’s strategic objectives.
