# History

E-JUST Library began its journey in 2011, originally located in Building 8 at the university’s secondary campus, with a modest collection of books generously donated by JICA. In 2016, the library was relocated to Building 13 and named Al Araby Library in honor of the generous funding provided by Al Araby Group for its furnishing. In 2020, the library moved to two temporary locations at the university’s main campus—Building 8 for postgraduate students and Building 9 for undergraduate students. In early 2022, the library settled into its current and permanent home, Fayza Aboulnaga Central Library, named in recognition of her remarkable contributions to E-JUST Board of Trustees.
