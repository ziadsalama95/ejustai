# Admissions & Application Process

## Undergraduate Admissions
- **Eligibility:** Egyptian Thanaweya Amma (Science/Math tracks depending on faculty), STEM schools, IG/IB, American Diploma, and equivalent international certificates.
- **Entrance Assessment:** Applicants typically take E-JUST admission exams (Mathematics, Physics/Chemistry or Computing depending on track, and English proficiency) and an interview.
- **Application Portal:** Online via the official E-JUST admissions portal (`https://www.ejust.edu.eg`).

## Postgraduate Admissions (M.Sc. & Ph.D.)
- **Requirements:** Accredited Bachelor's degree (for M.Sc.) or Master's degree (for Ph.D.) in a relevant field, strong academic GPA, English proficiency (TOEFL/IELTS or E-JUST English test), research proposal, and two academic recommendation letters.
- **Selection:** Academic evaluation followed by a technical interview with the department committee.

## Required Documents
1. Official academic transcripts and graduation certificate (or latest school certificate).
2. Copy of National ID or Passport.
3. Personal photo.
4. English proficiency proof (if available).
5. Updated CV and Statement of Purpose (for postgraduate applicants).
