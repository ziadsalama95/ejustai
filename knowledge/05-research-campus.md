# Research & Campus Life

## Research Focus Areas
- **Artificial Intelligence, Robotics & Smart Systems:** Computer vision, NLP, autonomous robotics, and embedded AI.
- **Renewable Energy & Sustainability:** Solar energy, water desalination, green hydrogen, and environmental engineering.
- **Advanced Materials & Nanotechnology:** Graphene, smart polymers, and industrial materials.
- **Biotechnology & Healthcare Engineering:** Bioinformatics, biomedical devices, and pharmaceutical engineering.

## Centers of Excellence
- Center of Excellence in Graphene and Nanotechnology
- Center of Excellence in Energy and Environment
- Smart Systems and Robotics Laboratories

## Campus Life & Student Services
- **Housing:** On-campus student dormitories for both male and female students with study rooms and dining facilities.
- **Transportation:** Shuttle buses connecting New Borg El Arab campus with Alexandria and nearby hubs.
- **Student Activities:** Robotics clubs, ACM/ICPC programming teams, entrepreneurship incubators, and cultural exchange programs with Japan.
