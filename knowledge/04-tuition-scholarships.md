# Tuition Fees & Scholarships

## Tuition Structure
- E-JUST is a national non-profit research university. Tuition fees vary by faculty (Engineering, CSIT, International Business) and student nationality (Egyptian vs. International).
- Fees are announced annually on the official E-JUST website prior to each academic year and can typically be paid in installments per semester.
- For exact current-year figures, always check the official E-JUST admissions/finance page at `https://www.ejust.edu.eg`.

## Scholarships & Financial Aid
1. **Merit & Excellence Scholarships:** Full or partial tuition waivers for top-performing Egyptian Thanaweya Amma, STEM, and international certificate students.
2. **TICAD / JICA African Scholarships:** Full scholarships for postgraduate students from African countries, covering tuition, accommodation, monthly stipend, and airfare.
3. **E-JUST Postgraduate Fellowships:** Research assistantships and tuition waivers for qualified M.Sc. and Ph.D. candidates working in funded research labs.
4. **Partner & Corporate Scholarships:** Sponsored seats supported by Egyptian ministries, banks, and industrial partners.
