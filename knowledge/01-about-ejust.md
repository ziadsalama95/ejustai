# About E-JUST

Egypt-Japan University of Science and Technology (E-JUST) is an Egyptian public research-oriented university established in partnership with the Japanese government through the Japan International Cooperation Agency (JICA).

## Location & Campus
- **Main Campus:** New Borg El Arab City, Alexandria Governorate, Egypt.
- **Distance:** Approximately 45 km southwest of Alexandria and near Borg El Arab International Airport.
- **Campus Style:** Eco-friendly smart campus designed in collaboration with Japanese architects, featuring modern laboratories, central library, student dormitories, sports facilities, and green spaces.

## Mission & Vision
- E-JUST combines the Japanese model of research-based education (Kouza / laboratory-centered learning) with Egyptian academic excellence.
- It focuses on science, engineering, computer science, international business, and sustainable technology, serving Egypt, Africa, and the Middle East.
- Instruction is primarily in **English**, with Japanese language and culture courses integrated into the curriculum.
